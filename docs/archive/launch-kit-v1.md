# 1. Application form

## Solo + AI Berlin — Founding Circle Application

**Build independently. Learn together.**

Hi, I’m Alexey, a Berlin-based founder. I’ve run my own business since 2020. It has been profitable for about four and a half years, and I’ve been fully self-employed for about three and a half years.

I’m still launching new things and experimenting with how AI can help me build and run a business independently. I’d like a small group of people in Berlin to compare notes with as we do the work.

I’m putting together **10–12 founding members** for an English-speaking peer group focused on independent businesses and practical AI use. We’ll meet twice a month, ideally in Mitte or Prenzlauer Berg, with a private group chat between sessions.

This is a peer group, not a course. I’ll host and contribute as a fellow founder. Members will bring real experiments, workflows, questions, wins and failures. No one is expected to have all the answers, but everyone should have something to contribute.

### Who it’s for

The main focus is solopreneurs, independent founders, freelancers building repeatable businesses, and owners of intentionally small businesses. You should already be building, running or seriously testing something—not just collecting ideas.

You don’t need to be an AI expert, have a large audience, or be working on your business full-time. Public work is useful context, not an entry requirement. If you don’t fit the description exactly but believe you would contribute, please apply and tell me why.

### Why it’s paid

**Member-funded, not sponsor-led.** I want us to be able to rent a private room and set our own agenda, without depending on sponsors or product pitches.

The initial fee will fund the venue, basic running costs and a modest buffer. The aim is to build something useful and sustainable. Any future membership offer or pricing will be communicated separately.

### The pilot

**€59 total for two in-person sessions with the same group, plus a private group chat during the pilot.** Each session will last about 2.5 hours. The pilot will run over approximately one month, with no automatic renewal.

Exact dates, venue and payment terms will be shared before you are asked to pay. Applying is free, does not reserve a seat and does not create a payment obligation.

# 7. Pilot session structure

18:30–18:45 — Brief progress round: what each person is building and one current question.

18:45–19:15 — Two show-and-tells: 10 minutes each plus 5 minutes of questions.

19:15–19:25 — Break.

19:25–20:25 — Two focused business problem discussions, or parallel small-group sessions.

20:25–20:45 — Feedback and selecting the next practical experiment.

20:45–21:00 — Commitments, offers of help and closing.

Session one: surface current work and choose experiments. Session two: bring back what happened and decide whether to continue.

## Weekly chat prompt

**Shipped:** What did you put into the world or improve?

**Learned:** What changed your mind?

**Stuck:** What is one specific thing you need help with?

**Can help with:** What can you offer another member this week?

Participation should be useful, not performative. No daily posting requirement. No recording or AI transcription by default; obtain explicit permission before using other members’ private information in tools or public material.
