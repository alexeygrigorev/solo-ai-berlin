## 2. Cost-recovery model

Illustration from the discussion, not a quote or tax determination. Assumptions: two meetings per month; juggleHUB booked for three hours per meeting, or FreiRaum up to four hours; €50/month refreshments plus €50/month incidental-expense reserve; payment cost budgeted at 3% of receipts (not a quoted provider rate). Host time and income/profit taxes excluded.

The model assumes 19% VAT on membership and eligible input-VAT deduction for juggleHUB. No input-VAT deduction assumed for FreiRaum because published tax treatment was unclear. The actual operator's regime must be confirmed separately.

| Scenario | Monthly budget before payment fees | Gross fee at 10 paying members | At 12 | At 15 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FreiRaum, two meetings | €260 | €33 | €27 | €22 |
| juggleHUB, two × three hours | €394 | €49 | €41 | €33 |

Formula used: required gross receipts = budget / (1 / 1.19 - 0.03); divide by paying-member count and round up. These are planning numbers, not an accounting model. Unquoted after-hours surcharges are excluded.

At €59 for the two-session pilot, ten people produce €590 gross receipts, leaving approximately €84 after assumed output VAT, the 3% payment-cost allowance and €394 budget. Twelve leave approximately €180. This is not meaningful organiser compensation yet. With a confirmed €80 FreiRaum booking and no hidden extras, a €35–39 two-session offer was discussed as a near-cost alternative, but the latest draft keeps one €59 pilot offer.

## 4. Personal invitation draft (after an introduction or appropriate contact)

> Hey [Name] — I came across your work on [specific project or topic], and [specific reason it connects to this idea].
>
> I'm putting together a small Berlin peer group for people building independent businesses with AI: 10–12 members, meeting twice a month in English. Real workflows, experiments and business questions—not lectures or sales pitches.
>
> I'll host and participate as a fellow founder. The pilot would be €59 for two sessions, so we can rent a private room without relying on sponsors.
>
> Would you be open to taking a look at the outline? I'd value your perspective on whether this would be useful for you.
>
> — Alexey

For community organisers:

> I'd love to compare notes and understand whether this complements what you already run. Would you be open to a brief conversation?

Add the agreed November dates and application URL before using. Do not imply someone agreed to participate, endorse or partner.
