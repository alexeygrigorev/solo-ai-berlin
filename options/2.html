You don’t need a successful business yet, a large audience, or advanced AI skills. You do need something you’re actively working on.

02

AI in the actual work.

You’re using AI in your business or testing a practical way to apply it. An AI product isn’t required. A concrete workflow or experiment is.

+

02 / The format

A peer group.
Not another course.

No stage. No teacher–student split. A consistent group working through real decisions, sharing what works—and what doesn’t.

Bring the work

Show something real.

An AI workflow, a product experiment, a customer test, or a process you’ve improved. A rough demo is more useful here than a polished pitch.

Think together

Get specific feedback.

Work through a business challenge with people who have different experience. Ask questions, challenge assumptions, and offer practical help.

Come back to it

Try. Learn. Compare.

Choose a next experiment. Use the private group chat between sessions. Meet again two weeks later and share what actually happened.

Everyone comes to learn. Everyone brings something.

No unsolicited selling. No recording or AI transcription without everyone’s permission.

06 / Good questions

Before you apply.

Do I need a profitable business or an AI product?

No. A concrete business project and real action matter more than revenue. You might be testing a service, validating an idea, or running an established business. You don’t need to build an AI product, but you should be using AI or testing a specific application of it in your work.

I’m new to AI. Is there a place for me?

Yes, if you’re already trying it on a real business task and can bring that experience or a specific challenge. Your business experience is valuable, too. This pilot isn’t introductory training for people who only want to watch and listen.

What does contributing actually mean?

Attend both sessions, bring at least one concrete example from your own work during the pilot, and offer thoughtful feedback to others. A failed experiment or a carefully framed question counts. You don’t need a presentation, a big public profile, or an outgoing personality.

Why do I pay before my application is reviewed?

We’re forming a small, committed group, not collecting expressions of interest. Submit your answers and pay €59 + VAT to complete your application. Alexey reviews the fit based on an active business project, practical AI use, and willingness to contribute. Payment is an advance payment for participation, not a non-refundable application fee. Your place is confirmed only after approval and confirmation that the pilot will run.

What if my application is declined or the pilot doesn’t run?

You receive a full refund, with no processing fees deducted. This also applies if we cannot offer you a seat because the group is full. We need at least 10 approved, paid participants and a confirmed venue by 3 November 2026, 18:00 CET. If either is missing, we cancel and refund everyone. We initiate these refunds within five business days of the decision; your bank may take additional time to show the money.

What if I need to cancel, or the dates change?

You can cancel for a full refund until 3 November 2026, 18:00 CET. After that, the pilot is a two-session package: there is no voluntary refund for a missed session, unless we can fill your place with another suitable participant. If we change a date or move outside the stated area, you can decline the change for a full refund. Any statutory cancellation, withdrawal, and refund rights remain unaffected. See the pilot terms for the full conditions.

Where is it, and what happens after November?

Both evenings are planned for a private room in Mitte or Prenzlauer Berg. We’ll confirm the address and access information by 3 November. The private chat will be available during the November pilot; the platform will be confirmed with the group. After the two sessions we’ll decide whether to continue. Any future dates or fees will be offered separately, with no automatic subscription.

Will sessions be recorded or shared with AI tools?

Not by default. No recording, AI transcription, publication of another member’s story, or use of their private material in AI tools without their explicit permission. Please anonymise customer information and only share material you have the right to share.
