06 / Questions

Before you apply

Do I need a profitable business or an AI product?

You need a project and some work on it. You might be testing a service, checking an idea, or running a company already. You don’t need to build an AI product, but you should be using AI on the work or testing a specific use.

I’m new to AI. Is there a place for me?

Yes, if you’re already trying it on a real task and can bring that, or a specific question. This isn’t a class for people who only want to watch.

What does contributing mean?

Come to both evenings. Bring at least one example from your own work, and give useful comments to others. A failed test or a clear question is enough. You don’t need a presentation or a public profile.

Why do I pay before you review the application?

We’re putting together a small group, not a waitlist. You send the answers and pay €59 + VAT. I then look at the project, how you use AI, and whether you’ll take part. Payment is an advance for the two evenings, not a fee for reading the form. A place is confirmed only after I approve you and we confirm the pilot will run.

What if you decline, or the pilot doesn’t run?

You get a full refund, with no processing fees. Same if the group is full. We need at least 10 approved, paid participants and a confirmed room by 3 November 2026, 18:00 CET. If either is missing, I cancel and refund everyone. I start those refunds within five business days, though your bank may take longer to show the money.

What if I need to cancel, or the dates change?

You can get a full refund until 3 November 2026, 18:00 CET. After that, the two evenings are one package, and there’s no refund for a missed evening unless we can fill the place. If I change a date or move outside Mitte / Prenzlauer Berg, you can refuse the change and get a full refund. Statutory rights stay as they’re written. See the pilot terms.

Where is it, and what happens after November?

Both evenings are planned for a private room in Mitte or Prenzlauer Berg, and we’ll send the address by 3 November. There’s a group chat during the pilot. After the two evenings we decide whether to continue. Any later dates or fees are a separate offer, with no automatic subscription.

Will you record sessions or put them into AI tools?

Not unless everyone agrees. Don’t record, transcribe with AI, publish someone else’s story, or put their private material into AI tools without asking. Anonymise customer information, and only share what you have the right to share.
